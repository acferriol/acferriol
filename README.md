# Alejandro Cespón Ferriol

**AI/ML researcher · PhD student at the University of Granada · DaSCI**

I work on explainable artificial intelligence and large language models, with a background in machine learning, convex optimization, and backend development. I am interested in applied research roles that connect experimental methods with useful software.

Based in Granada, Spain. Open to conversations about AI/ML research, research engineering, and applied data science opportunities.
